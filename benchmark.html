The model, in plain English

The price of the tokens
the market actually consumes.

Give each model’s token price a weight equal to its share of aggregate market token usage. Add those weighted prices. The result is the price of one common token for that market and period.

Input 01 / The market’s consumptionq

Aggregate market usage.

How many qualifying tokens of each model the whole defined market consumes—not one buyer’s usage or preferred basket.

Model’s market tokens÷All market tokens=Usage share
Input 02 / The market’s pricesp

Open-market token prices.

What a token from each qualifying model costs in the market, expressed on a consistent pricing basis—not the cost of a particular GPU or a score for model quality.

Model token→Observable standardized price
Put the two together

CCBT price=Sum of (model price × market token usage)Total qualifying market tokens consumed

The same idea, in mathematics
PCCBT,t=Σi pi,t qi,tΣi qi,t=Σi wi,t pi,t
p = standardized price per model tokenq = model-token usage across the defined marketw = q ÷ total market-token usage

Market usage sets the weights. Your allocation comes later. A model serving 40% of qualifying market tokens carries 40% of the weight. The result is usage-weighted, not an equal average of available models.

Conversion rules and what this example assumes

Purchase and consumption are separate.

All quantities are measured in millions. With purchase-date reference P₀ and budget B: N = B ÷ P₀. At consumption date T, a model priced at pᵢ,T costs pᵢ,T ÷ Pₜ CCBT per token. An allocation share aᵢ therefore yields N × aᵢ × Pₜ ÷ pᵢ,T million model tokens.

This is not a forward quote.

The demo assumes a prepaid, index-linked entitlement, priced at the selected spot reference and carried without fees. It does not model financing, forward premiums, basis, capacity limits, execution, credit, or delivery guarantees. Same-date conversion is ordinary budget allocation.

“Market token mix” is price-aware.

To reproduce the market’s token shares wᵢ, CCBT allocation shares must be aᵢ = wᵢ × pᵢ ÷ Pₜ. Simply copying usage percentages into the spending pie would produce a different token mix. The preset uses unrounded values.

Availability and rounding.

Only models with positive usage are eligible in this fictional scenario. Presets refresh when dates change; a custom basket keeps surviving allocations and redistributes exited-model shares proportionally. New models start at zero in a custom basket. Calculations use full precision; displayed numbers are rounded. Nothing here places a trade.

All values belong to the same fictional representative segment. There is no live market connection.
