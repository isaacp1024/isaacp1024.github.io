01 / The problem

One Business Input.
A Fragmented Market.

Businesses consume AI, but buy it through model-specific tokens, provider-specific prices, and hardware-specific capacity. Every model has its own economics—and the mix keeps changing.

Bills measure spending. Native tokens measure individual models. A common economic unit could connect consumption, cost-benefit analysis, and planning as models and prices change.

Why the gap remains

Different tools.
The same missing denominator.

Access & routing

API aggregators.

A shared interface simplifies access and routing. It does not, by itself, give different model tokens a common economic denomination.

Hardware capacity

GPU markets & futures.

A GPU contract prices specified hardware capacity. It does not follow the changing models and token costs through which AI is consumed.

Bills & budgets

Spend tools & credits.

Bills and prepaid credits organize expenditure. They do not define a market-wide, usage-linked unit for AI consumption.

These tools alone do not give the following questions one common basis
01 / Model consumption

How does spend change as usage grows and the model mix shifts?

02 / Compare cost & benefit

Is a model’s higher cost worth the additional value to our business?

03 / Plan through change

How do we budget and commit without freezing today’s choices?

What could give these questions a shared economic language?

02 / The concept

A New Universal Standard
for AI Token Denomination.

We propose a solution to this market gap: a generic economic token in which the costs of other model tokens can be expressed.

Ground the reference in aggregate market usage and model-token prices. As models enter, leave, and gain share, the basis evolves. Analysis and contracts can use the same economic language.

03 / Our proposal

CCBT Is the Denomination.
Standard Basis Is the Market.

Two complementary pieces: a standard for the economic unit, and a company building the indexing, brokerage, and market infrastructure around it.

The denomination & mathematical standardΣ

CCBT

Common Compute Basis Tokens

A proposed universal denomination. Its reference price combines qualifying model-token prices, weighted by their shares of aggregate market consumption. The basis follows the market—not one buyer’s basket.
