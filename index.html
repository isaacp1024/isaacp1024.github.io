The Market for AI Compute.

Standard Basis is developing a marketplace concept for AI compute around CCBT — a model- and compute-independent denomination designed to make heterogeneous AI consumption comparable, contractible, and deliverable across providers and through time.

01 / The problem

AI Has Prices. It Doesn’t Have a Common Basis.

Model tokens are specific to individual models. GPU-hours are specific to particular hardware. Both models and hardware change rapidly.

A business can know it will consume substantial AI five years from now without knowing which models it will use, what hardware will run them, or what those models will cost. That makes AI difficult to compare today — and exceptionally difficult to price, budget, or contract through time.

The market needs a unit that can survive the technologies it measures.

02 / Common basis

A Common Compute Basis Token.

CCBT is a proposed common economic denomination for AI consumption. For a defined model market, it represents the utilization-weighted price of the tokens that market actually consumes. As usage changes, the composition of the common basis changes with it.

03 / Two-sided market

One Market. Many Models. Many Providers.

Standard Basis is proposed as a two-sided brokerage and marketplace: buyers describe the AI consumption they need; compute providers compete to serve that demand; Standard Basis standardizes requests, compares offers on a common basis, and facilitates transactions.

04 / Deliverable CCBT

Buy the AI. Choose the Model Later.

A company may know how much AI it expects to consume in five years. It cannot know which model it will want to use. A deliverable CCBT contract is proposed to separate those decisions.

The buyer fixes a quantity and price basis for future AI consumption without choosing a contemporary model or hardware generation.

05 / Market data

A Market Creates Its Own Benchmark.

Every request reveals demand. Every provider quote reveals supply. Every transaction reveals a clearing price. In concept, brokerage activity can generate the evidence needed to strengthen CCBT spot and forward price discovery.

06 / Long-term vision

Price AI Through Time.
