01 / What we are building

The Market Around
a Common Standard.

Standard Basis is the company developing a brokerage and maintained reference indices for AI consumption. CCBT is the denomination those indices apply.

The initial business

Help counterparties transact.

Bring buyers and providers together, make requirements and offers comparable, and help structure the terms. Useful procurement is the starting point—not an assumption that a liquid exchange already exists.

03 / The basis through time

Tomorrow’s Market.
Not Today’s Basket.

The denomination stays the same. The models, usage weights, and price do not. At each date, CCBT references the market then—not a frozen collection of today’s tokens.

For a proposed future commitment, the agreed CCBT quantity can be allocated among qualifying models at delivery. The current market basket determines the reference; it does not dictate your eventual allocation.

What carries forward?

The agreed CCBT quantity, defined market, and delivery terms—not a fixed dollar value, native token count, or set of models.

What changes with the market?

Qualifying models, aggregate usage weights, the reference price, and conversion into each model’s tokens.

04 / The path ahead

From Procurement
to a Liquid Market.

Start with useful transactions. Build the shared evidence, standards, and counterparty relationships that can support a broader market.

01 / Commercial starting point

Comparable procurement.

Standardized requests and competing quotes connect real buying needs to supply. Participants can see which terms make a difference.

02 / A stronger common basis

Reference data & analysis.

Observed prices and representative market usage support maintained indices, model conversions, and a record of changing consumption.

03 / A broader market

Commitments through time.

Repeatable terms could support future price discovery, financing, risk transfer, and secondary markets for sufficiently standardized positions.

Liquidity is an outcome to build toward, not a property supplied by the formula. Each step depends on participation, credible data, delivery arrangements, and appropriate governance.
